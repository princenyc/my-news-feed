The Algorithm

The Signal uses a weighted engine to prioritize "High-Signal" news. Freshness Boosts (+500) ensure breaking news stays at the top, while stories older than 48 hours receive a Recency Penalty (-400) to keep the feed current.

What is the "IMPACT" tag?

The IMPACT badge is automatically applied to stories that mention structural levers of power, such as legislation, court rulings, voting policy, or institutional accountability. These stories are given a +300 point boost to ensure systemic news isn't buried by culture or fluff.
