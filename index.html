How it Works

The Signal uses a weighted engine to prioritize news. Impact Boosts (+300) are given to stories about policy and legislation. Freshness Boosts (+500) ensure breaking news stays at the top. PR Penalties (-150) bury generic releases.

What is the "IMPACT" tag?

The IMPACT badge marks stories mentioning structural levers of power like legislation or court rulings. These get a +300 point boost to ensure they aren't buried.
